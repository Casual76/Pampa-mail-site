Pampa Mail – Privacy Policy

Pampa Mail is an Android email client developed by Alessio Pampaloni.

What the app accesses

When you add a Google account, Pampa Mail asks for permission to read and modify your Gmail messages (Gmail API, scope gmail.modify) so that it can show, search, organize, draft and send your email. When you add a Microsoft account, it asks for the equivalent Microsoft Graph permissions.

How the data is used

Your email data is used only to provide the email features you see in the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and it is never sold.

Where the data is stored

Messages and account access tokens are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Pampa Mail has no server and the developer cannot see your email.

Sharing

Your data is not shared with anyone. The only exception is an optional AI assistant: if you explicitly turn it on and choose a provider, the text you ask it to process is sent to that provider with your own key, and only then.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Pampa Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Removing an account in the app or uninstalling it deletes all locally stored data. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account or Microsoft account security settings.
